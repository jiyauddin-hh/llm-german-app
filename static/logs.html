How to read this log
  1. 🔌 Connect: the learner's app opens a session and says which chapter, lesson and mode.
  2. 🔑 Login: the tutor asks the German app's server whether the learner's login is valid.
  3. ⏱ Limits: checks there is a free slot and the learner has minutes left today.
  4. 📘 Lesson: loads the lesson's words and sentences for Mira to teach.
  5. 🎤 Hear: what the learner typed, or what speech recognition heard them say.
  6. 🧠 Think / 💬 Reply: Mira's answer, how long it took, and the German in it.
  7. 🔊 Voice: each sentence turned into speech. xtts is the natural voice, piper the robotic backup.
  8. 🏁 End: why the session ended, turns, XP and length.

Green rows went well, orange rows are worth a look, red rows are problems. took is how long that step made the learner wait. Each session has a badge (S1, S2, …) so you can follow one learner. The log is kept in memory: the last 2000 events, cleared when the server restarts.
